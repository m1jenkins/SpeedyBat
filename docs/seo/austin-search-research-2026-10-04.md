## Local Google work that website code cannot complete

**REQUIRED / documented:** Google explains that local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence; searcher location affects distance. A complete profile helps Google understand the business, and real reviews and links contribute to prominence. This makes “front page for anyone, anywhere” an unsuitable guarantee. [Google local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).

1. Owner verifies the existing Google Business Profile or creates an eligible one if none exists. Use the real-world name **Speedy Bat Couriers**, the controlled dispatch number **(512) 910-4938**, canonical website, a courier-relevant primary category available in the profile, and verified services. Use the fewest categories needed; do not append keywords to the business name. Profile state is not verified here. [Google business representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en).
2. If customers do not visit a staffed, signed business location, use the service-area model and hide the address. Do not use a virtual office to obtain a downtown location or open profiles for the destination cities. Google generally allows one profile for a service-area business; additional locations require genuine separate locations/staff under its guidance. [Business representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en).
3. Describe real local pickup coverage, rather than treating every long-distance destination as local service coverage. Google supports up to 20 named service areas, asks for specific accurate areas, and generally recommends boundaries within about two hours' driving time. A service area field is not a promise of ranking in each area. [Service-area guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481).
4. Choose operating hours with the owner based on the applicable business/category rules and actual customer-facing operation. A form accepting requests continuously does not establish 24/7 dispatch or delivery. Publish real fleet/team/work photos with consent.
5. Establish a consistent request for honest reviews after completed jobs, without incentives, filtering for satisfied customers, or asking for specific keyword text. Reply helpfully. Google's policy allows genuine review requests but prohibits incentives and selectively soliciting positive reviews. [Maps contribution policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114).
6. Reconcile the real name/phone/website/service facts across official profiles, legitimate courier directories, and genuine local business memberships. Prioritize existing accurate listings and meaningful relationships. Seek client/partner mentions based on real work; do not purchase ranking links. Off-site work here is a recommendation, not an executed account change.

## AI search and policy discipline

The official [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), updated July 10, 2026, confirms that normal SEO remains relevant, recommends original useful content, rejects special AI text/schema tactics, and points to the Generative AI performance report. The [Search generative AI control documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024) says the control rolled out worldwide on August 31, 2026, with Include as the default; this setting affects AI-feature inclusion and is not an ordinary Search ranking signal. Check the actual property and inherited setting with account access. The older [AI features page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), updated December 10, 2025, still describes AI traffic inside overall Web performance; use the newer guide for present report recommendations and verify the account UI. Account access and the setting itself remain unverified.

Keep useful answers and visible business facts in ordinary accessible HTML; apply truthful Organization/LocalBusiness/Service/Breadcrumb markup where relevant to the site. Treat schema as understanding and applicable feature eligibility, not a first-page or citation shortcut. Do not prioritize `llms.txt`, hidden instructions, or repetitive AI-optimized keyword pages over crawlability and real evidence. Google prohibits similar city/query doorway pages and keyword stuffing. [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

For a modest AI panel, repeat questions such as “Who can deliver a contract across Austin today?”, “How do I request an Austin airport cargo recovery?”, and “What do I need to quote a daily Austin business route?” across the engines that matter. Record date, exact prompt, platform, location/account settings, whether web retrieval occurred, cited URLs, business mentions, and whether the description matches Speedy Bat's actual scope. Compare rolling observations and qualified referrals separately from Google organic and Maps outcomes.
