Google observation

Query: courier service Austin. Google’s “Try without personalization” view labeled the results Austin, TX. Places showed Austin Express Couriers, 1-512-DELIVER, and Dropoff. Nine visible organic listings did not include Speedy Bat.

One October 4 snapshot; location, device, wording, and time affect results. It is not a citywide ranking baseline. Google's buying questions included courier charges and cheapest delivery.

Technical foundation

All 17 live sitemap pages returned 200 with readable initial HTML, one matching canonical, one H1, and structured data. Sampled host, slash, retired-page, and unknown-page behavior was correct.

Search Console indexing, profile verification, reviews, actual search volumes, qualified organic leads, and mobile field performance remain unverified.

Two visibility targets: the website competes for organic results; the Google Business Profile competes in Places and Maps. Both lead to requests that dispatch must qualify. First-page placement is an objective to measure, not a promise for every searcher.
